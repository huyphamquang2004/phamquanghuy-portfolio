01 / A little about me

Behind every number,
a business story.

I work at the intersection of business, finance and data. As an SME Relationship Manager at MSB's Nam Thang Long Branch, I focus on understanding how businesses operate, how cash moves, and where the right financing can make a difference.

My foundation comes from International Economics at the Diplomatic Academy of Vietnam, investment experience at MBV, and hands-on exposure to logistics. Each has shaped how I connect the financial picture with the realities of a business.

I've passed CFA Level I and am studying for Level II. Alongside a DELF B2 French certification, I continue developing my toolkit in Excel, SQL and Power BI — with curiosity at the centre of it all.
